## A power-state change no longer stops the panel (pull request 29, issue 27)

- 0.2.7 stopped with `EXC_BREAKPOINT` in `dispatch_assert_queue_fail` when Low Power
  Mode changed (on a laptop set to use it on battery, plugging or unplugging the
  charger does this). Foundation posts `NSProcessInfoPowerStateDidChange` on a queue of
  its own; the main-actor `@objc environmentChanged` entry checked its isolation there
  and trapped. It is now `nonisolated` and moves to the main actor before
  `updateMotion()`.
- The report's frame named `statusItemClicked`: in the release build five identical
  `@objc` main-actor entries (statusItemClicked, environmentChanged, togglePanel,
  toggleFloating, toggleMenuBar) are merged into one body named after the first, and
  `environmentChanged` tail-branches into it with its own line number (602).
- No other observer is delivered off the main queue: NSWorkspace, screen-parameter and
  distributed notifications, event monitors, timers, and the config watcher all arrive
  on main.
- Checked: `scripts/check.sh`; the 0.2.7 release binary's disassembly; the fixed
  release build's thunk no longer checks its executor; a minimal Swift 6 program with
  the same shape traps before the change and runs after it; the contributor's Mac
  Studio survives toggling Low Power Mode on the fixed build, 0.2.7 does not.
- Not checked: a laptop's charger; that motion turns off in Low Power Mode.
