# 2026-10-31

## The whole machine's glyph is the kind of Mac

- Built on `feat/machine-icons` of a contributor's fork, from Nongfsq/ftop `main` at
  `a318876`. The decision doc's section "The whole machine's glyph is the kind of Mac"
  holds what was built and why.
- Before: every Mac drew a laptop beside the whole machine's watts, a Mac Studio
  included.
- `Snapshot.productName` carries the firmware's name, read once by the sampler;
  `MachineKind` in `FtopCore` takes the kind from its start; `Glyph.machine` takes the
  kind. The power hover names the Mac, and `ftop doctor` has a `machine` line.
- Checked: `scripts/check.sh`; reference images regenerated, and the new
  `writeMachineImages` render (each kind, light and dark) looked at; `ftop doctor` on a
  Mac Studio (M5 Max) reads "Mac Studio (M5 Max), drawn as studio"; the installed panel
  on that Mac, seen by the contributor: the Mac Studio glyph before the watts, and the
  hover "Mac Studio (M5 Max) · 整机功耗 17.4 W · 电源输入 16.3 W · GPU 0.7 W".
- Not checked: any Mac other than the
  Mac Studio, so the MacBook, Mac mini, iMac, and Mac Pro names are the forms Apple
  uses, not read from those machines; the corner and vertical-strip layouts, which
  draw the same glyph through the same code.
