# 2026-10-31

## GPU usage is busy time weighted by clock (plan `docs/plans/2026-10-31-gpu-usage-clock-weighted.md`)

- Built by a contributor on the owner's delegation; the decision doc's amendment "GPU
  usage is busy time weighted by clock" holds what was decided and why, and
  `docs/evidence/2026-10-28-gpu-usage/` the measurements behind it. The contributor found
  the GPU figure high at rest: on an M5 Max the GPU composites two 5K displays busy
  20–50 % of the time at 338 MHz and under 1 W, and the driver's `Device Utilization %` showed that
  busy share. The figure is now busy share × weighted frequency ÷ top frequency, from the
  `GPUPH` residencies and `perf-states` table the frequency line already used
  (`GPUSample.usage(busy:mhz:maxMHz:driver:)` in `FtopCore`). When the clock is unknown
  it is the busy share alone, and when IOReport gives nothing it is the driver's figure;
  `ftop doctor` names which. The hover adds the busy share, `probe` prints it, and
  `probe --json` gains `busy` and `usageSource`. Nothing else on the panel changed.
- No new system reads: the sampler already read everything the new figure needs, so
  there is no cost table. The driver is still read each sample, for memory and as the
  last fallback.
- Checked (M5 Max, one 5K display that day):
  - `scripts/check.sh` with Xcode's toolchain; the new `GPUUsageTests` cover the
    handoff's rest and full-load numbers, the true 0, both fallbacks, and the clamp.
  - Reference images: all 98 rendered from `main` and from the branch are byte-identical.
    The sample snapshot keeps usage 0.37 (now busy 0.94 at 620 of 1578 MHz), and the
    hover is not part of the reference images.
  - `ftop probe` at rest: GPU 7–8 %, busy 34–37 %, 0.34 GHz, 0.8 W. Under
    `stress.swift`: GPU 100 %, busy 100 %, 1.62 GHz, about 84 W. `doctor` reads "busy
    time weighted by clock, from IOReport (GPUPH and perf-states)".
  - With the IOReport sampler forced to fail (a local edit, reverted): GPU 40 % from
    the driver, `usageSource` `driver`, busy unavailable, and `doctor` says so.
  - The real panel, built with `scripts/bundle.sh` and launched from `build/` with the
    GPU module on (the installed 0.2.7 quit for the run and restarted after): at rest
    the column sits far below its line (8–9 % against a line at about a fifth);
    under `stress.swift` the column fills to the top and meets the line; the menu bar's
    GPU reading followed (8–10 %, then 100 %). The contributor dragged the panel
    through the plain strip, the wide strip, the vertical strip, the corner, compact,
    full, and detailed layouts; the GPU hover was captured in each layout that has
    the GPU, e.g. `GPU · 8% · busy 39% · 0.34 GHz · 0.7 W · VRAM 1.6 GB · 37°` in the
    corner and `GPU · 6% · busy 24% · 0.41 GHz · 0.5 W · VRAM 1.4 GB · 35°` in the
    vertical strip. The plain strip has no GPU, as before.
- Not checked: M1–M4 and M6 hardware (only the M5 Max's `GPUPH` active share has been
  read for usage; the M6's states past its table should fall back to the busy share);
  two displays with this build; graphics-heavy or memory-heavy loads (only the FMA
  compute load); the busy-share fallback on real hardware (covered by tests only); the
  Chinese hover text on the real panel; `scripts/perf.sh`.
