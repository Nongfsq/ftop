## Own cost at the largest layout (release build, 1442×643, two process columns, all users)

Measured with `top` on this machine, panel showing:

- Before: about 4% of one core, 65 MB. A time profile put most of it in redrawing
  process rows: every row whose figure changed re-scaled and color-matched its app
  icon, and Core Animation converted the whole row image again on commit.
- App icons are now scaled once per size and copied (`AppIcons.image(for:pixels:)`):
  about 1.4%.
- A row is now two layers, icon and name drawn once, the figure in a small layer of its
  own with room for the widest figure: main-thread work fell from about 0.9% to about
  0.4%, the panel to a mean of 1.1% over 30 s, 58 MB. The helper adds about 0.5%.
- Still over the 1% budget at this size. What is left is mostly the sampler (the
  process batch from the helper, the temperature and frequency reads).
- The controller's key types are asked for once instead of on every read, and a window
  size change around an unchanged scene only moves what is drawn: mean 0.91% over 60 s,
  52 MB, inside the budget at the largest layout. Smaller layouts were not measured.
